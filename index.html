[Some Notes on the Closure of Most Pages on zhedonghu.github.io]

To visitors:

I am sorry, but today I have decided to stop public access to most pages on zhedonghu.github.io.

zhedonghu.github.io contains some rather humorous and witty expressions, and also hosts a few simple mini-games that I casually made with vibe coding. This may stray from technical research itself and affect other students' learning.

Since a small number of pages are still publicly accessible, I have created a list  to show which pages remain available; any page not on the list has stopped being publicly accessible.

It is still uncertain whether the closed pages will be made public again.

Of course, I will continue to take part in open-source projects with undiminished enthusiasm. As of this writing, I am still working on a new project.

Thank you for your understanding!

Sincerely,
